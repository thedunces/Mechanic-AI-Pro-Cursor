Privacy Policy — Mechanic AI Pro

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mechanic AI Pro collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Vehicle information (make, model, year, VIN, engine, trim)
  • Diagnostic information (OBD-II codes, live data parameters, symptoms, notes)
  • Account information if you choose to link an email or Google account
  • Subscription entitlement and monthly AI-usage counts

Information Collected Automatically

  • App usage analytics via Firebase Analytics
  • Crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics
  • Device information required for authentication and app security (Firebase App Check)

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide AI-powered diagnostic recommendations
  • Store your vehicle history and past diagnoses
  • Improve app reliability and performance
  • Secure the app against abuse (App Check)

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use third-party services to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase for authentication, database, analytics, crash reporting, and cloud functions
  • Google Gemini API (via Cloud Functions) to generate diagnostic responses
  • NHTSA vPIC API for vehicle validation
  • Google Play Billing for subscription purchases, renewals, restoration, and fraud prevention

4. Data Security

We use Firebase Authentication, App Check, server-side subscription verification, and Firestore Security Rules to restrict access to your data. Entitlements and usage limits cannot be changed by the Android client.

5. Your Choices

  • You can use the app as a guest without linking an external account.
  • A durable email or Google account is required before buying Pro so the subscription can be restored.
  • You can manage or cancel a Pro subscription through Google Play.
  • You can delete your account and associated vehicles, diagnosis history, and usage records from Settings. Active Google Play subscriptions must be canceled in Google Play.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact support@mechanicai.pro.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy in the app and at this URL.
